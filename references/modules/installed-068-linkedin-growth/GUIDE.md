# linkedin-growth

## Finalidade

The LinkedIn personal-profile growth playbook for 2026. Use when someone wants to "grow on LinkedIn," "get more LinkedIn followers/reach," "a LinkedIn strategy," "how the LinkedIn algorithm works," or how to use commenting/newsletters/video/connections to grow. Dwell time + early meaningful comments are the engine, personal profiles massively out-reach company pages, links go in the first comment, and strategic commenting on target accounts is the cheapest reach on the platform. Uses the LADDER framework. Reads brand-profile + voice-builder first. The agent drafts posts + comment copy and designs the system; a human does the live commenting/connecting/DMs; WoopSocial schedules posts; metrics come from native LinkedIn analytics (SSI as a directional check); nothing is fabricated. Single-post craft → linkedin-post-writer; company page → linkedin-company-pages.
