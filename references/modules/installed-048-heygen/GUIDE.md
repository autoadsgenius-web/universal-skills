# heygen

## Finalidade

The AI avatar / talking-head mini-skill (HeyGen). Use when someone wants an "AI avatar video," "talking-head video," "digital twin / clone of myself on camera," "faceless presenter video," "spokesperson video," or to "translate/localize a video into many languages with lip-sync." Scripts and sets up the video; HeyGen renders; the human reviews/edits; WoopSocial schedules/ publishes. Sits below the ai-video router, sibling to veo-3. Consented avatars only; AI disclosure mandatory.
