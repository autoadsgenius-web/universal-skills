# youtube-seo-audit

## Finalidade

Advanced full YouTube channel audit with parallel sub-skill delegation. Analyzes topical authority, channel-level retention patterns, traffic- source mix, upload velocity consistency, playlist session chains, and sub-skill breakdowns. Generates health score and prioritized action plan. Use when user says "YouTube audit", "audit my channel", "full YouTube check", or provides a channel URL.
