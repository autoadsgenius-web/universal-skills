# youtube-shorts

## Finalidade

The growth engine for YouTube Shorts in 2026. Use when someone asks to "grow on Shorts," "get views on YouTube Shorts," "write a Shorts script," "why did my Short flop," "make a vertical YouTube video," or wants a Shorts strategy. Drafts the script; the human films/ edits; publishing routes through scheduling-and-queue - WoopSocial; analytics are read natively in YouTube Studio. Fully decoupled from, and sibling to, youtube-long-form.
