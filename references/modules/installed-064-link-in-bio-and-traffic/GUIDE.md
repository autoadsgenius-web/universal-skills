# link-in-bio-and-traffic

## Finalidade

Link-in-bio and traffic strategy — convert social reach into clicks, subscribers, and owned audience. Use when someone wants to "drive traffic from social," "optimize my link in bio," "get more clicks," "turn followers into email subscribers / leads / sales," "where do I put my link," or to convert reach into an owned audience. Turns rented reach into clicks and an OWNED audience: one clear action (not a link-dump), capture-before-sell, correct link placement per platform (in-post links are suppressed on X/LinkedIn/Facebook), and branded short links + UTM tracking. Uses the CLICK framework. Reads brand-profile + goals-and-kpis first. WoopSocial publishes the driving posts and places links where the API allows; the bio hub / landing page / email tool are external (3rd-party + human); clicks/conversions are read from those + GA4, never fabricated. Distinct from profile-optimization, social-selling-and-dm, email-and-newsletter, and lead-magnets-and-funnels.
